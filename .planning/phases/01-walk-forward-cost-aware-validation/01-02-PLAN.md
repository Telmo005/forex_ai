<objective>
Implement cost-aware backtesting (VALID-02): every simulated trade subtracts modeled spread, slippage, and commission from its `pnl_r` at the moment the trade closes, inside `run_hedge_backtest()`'s simulation loop — not bolted on downstream (RESEARCH.md Pattern 1). Thread the per-symbol cost params created in plan 01-01 through the strategy lab so no approval-relevant path is cost-blind, extend the registry to record the cost-model version, and make the dashboard show unambiguously that all displayed metrics are net-of-cost.

Per CLAUDE.md rule 4 and VALID-02's wording ("em toda validacao... nao so o gate manual"), placing cost logic inside the engine means any future automated gate (v2 AUTOGATE-01) inherits cost-aware numbers by construction. Costs are converted into the existing "R" unit (entry-spread-std-dev multiples) so they compose directly with `pnl_r` (RESEARCH.md Pattern 1, Pitfall 2).

Purpose: Close the cost-blindness gap named in `docs/strategy_lab_spec.md`'s "Limitacoes conhecidas" and satisfy VALID-02 end-to-end from engine to dashboard.
Output: A cost-aware `run_hedge_backtest`, an `apply_transaction_costs` helper, cost-threaded strategy generation, a `cost_model_version` registry column, and a net-of-cost dashboard.
</objective>
